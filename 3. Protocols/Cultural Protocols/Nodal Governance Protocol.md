## Principles

Not all decisions are equal in systemic impact, even among those with similar reversibility. A nodal decision is one where the intervention point creates cascading effects across multiple domains and membranes. The reversibility matrix determines speed. Nodal awareness determines depth.

This protocol draws on the living systems principle that systems evolve through leverage points — the places where minimal input generates maximal systemic benefit. Governance that identifies and acts at nodes is gardening. Governance that treats all decisions uniformly is administration.

## Identifying Nodal Moments

Agents and stewards should develop sensitivity to nodal decisions. A decision is nodal when it:

- **Sets precedent** — First instances of a decision type that all subsequent decisions will reference
- **Crosses structural boundaries** — Affects the relationship between membranes, not just within them
- **Alters relational patterns** — Changes how participants interact, not just what they do
- **Ripples through federation** — Impacts not only this commons but federated networks

### Common Nodal Decisions

| Decision Type | Why It's Nodal |
|--------------|----------------|
| First federation agreements | Set patterns that all subsequent federations reference |
| Agent scope expansions | Redefine the human-AI relationship within the commons |
| Schema standard changes | Ripple across the entire knowledge commons and all federated networks |
| Membrane redesigns | Alter the developmental pathway for all future participants |
| New treasury allocation categories | Create expectations that shape future resource flows |
| Accountability precedents | Define how the commons responds to future conflicts |

## Nodal Decision Process

When a decision is identified as nodal:

| Step | Action | Actor | Purpose |
|------|--------|-------|---------|
| 1 | Flag explicitly | Any participant or agent | "This is a nodal decision because [cascading effects]" |
| 2 | Stewardship confirms nodal status | [[Stewardship]] | Validates the flag based on cascading impact criteria |
| 3 | Expand deliberation window | Automatic | Minimum 72h regardless of reversibility classification |
| 4 | Invite cross-domain participation | Stewardship | All working circles notified, not just originating circle |
| 5 | Document rationale thoroughly | Proposer + community | This becomes precedent — future decisions will reference it |
| 6 | Standard consent process | [[Commons Assembly]] | 3 consents minimum, no unresolved paramount objections |
| 7 | Schedule retrospective | Stewardship | 30-day and 90-day review of systemic effects |

### Flagging

Any participant (human or agent) can flag a decision as nodal at any time during the consent window. Agents are particularly well-positioned to detect nodal patterns because they have visibility across all domains.

To flag: Post in the proposal thread with the format:
> "I'm flagging this as a nodal decision because [specific cascading effects across domains/membranes]."

### Dispute Resolution

If there is disagreement about whether a decision is nodal:
- Default to treating it as nodal (the cost of extra deliberation is low; the cost of missing a leverage point is high)
- Stewardship makes final determination

## Retrospective

Every nodal decision receives a scheduled retrospective:

**30-day retrospective:**
- What cascading effects have been observed?
- Were the anticipated impacts accurate?
- Are there unintended consequences to address?

**90-day retrospective:**
- How has this decision shaped subsequent decisions?
- Has the precedent been referenced, and was it helpful?
- Should the decision be amended based on observed systemic effects?

Retrospective findings are documented in the Knowledge Commons as precedent learnings.
